# SecondMind — 90-Second Demo Script

This demo presents **SecondLook**, the before-action checkpoint within the broader SecondMind thought-partner experience.

## 0–15 seconds: The trigger

Show a user reading a dismissive workplace email and drafting an angry reply:

> “As I already explained twice, this failure was caused by your team.”

Before sending, SecondMind offers its SecondLook prompt:

> **This message may escalate the conversation. Want a second look?**

## 15–45 seconds: System 2 reasoning

The user accepts. SecondMind shows:

- **Fact:** The recommendation was not followed.
- **Assumption:** The recipient ignored it deliberately.
- **Unknown:** Why a different decision was made.
- **Possible bias:** Anger is increasing certainty about intent.
- **Goal:** Resolve the issue while preserving accountability.

## 45–65 seconds: Counterfactuals

SecondMind compares three strategies:

1. **Send as written:** Fast and forceful; high escalation risk.
2. **Ask for context:** Lower conflict; may delay accountability.
3. **Firm and constructive:** States the evidence and requests a joint review.

The user selects option three and receives an editable draft.

## 65–80 seconds: User control and privacy

Show that raw conversation context is temporary and that long-term memory is off until the user approves an insight.

The user saves only:

> “In tense conversations, ask one clarifying question before assigning intent.”

## 80–90 seconds: Close

> “Most AI helps us produce answers faster. SecondMind helps us improve the thinking that comes before the answer.”
