Behavioral ground rules

These rules translate influential research traditions into product behavior. They are design constraints—not claims that SecondMind can diagnose people or always know the right answer.

Privacy and boundaries

Context is useful only when the user understands and controls how it is processed.

✓

Private by architecture

SecondMind minimizes context before reasoning, keeps raw conversations transient, and stores long-term insights only after explicit approval.

On-device context detectionRecognize decision moments without uploading the full conversation
Ask before offering a SecondLookNever block or silently rewrite the user’s action
User-approved memory onlySave abstractions, not transcripts or profiles of other people
Cloud conversation archiveOff by default; not required for this prototype
Safety boundary: SecondMind is not a therapist, crisis service, or source of clinical, legal, or financial advice. It should not infer diagnoses, manipulate emotions, or make consequential decisions for the user. When a situation exceeds its role, it should say so clearly and encourage appropriate human help.
